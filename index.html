How to deploy for iOS

  1. Create a free Apple Developer Account: Apple Developer
  2. Download and install SideStore on a PC/Mac with iPhone drivers or iTune: Side Store
  3. Login to SideStore with your Apple Developer account
  4. Connect your iPhone to your PC via USB cable and Trust the Computer
  5. Enable Developer Mode on your iPhone
  6. Deploy SideStore on your iPhone
  7. Launch SideStore on your iPhone
  8. Go to iPhone Settings>General>VPN & Device Management>Developer App and trust your Apple developer account
  9. Relaunch SideStore on your iPhone
  10. Connect with your Apple Developer Account
  11. Add a source: cedricmillard.github.io/MyAppStore/CMillardApps.json
  12. Open source and install the Apps
  13. Report Bugs to Cedric
